The Barons' War 2nd Edition – Quick Reference

General rules side • built for table use alongside the King John sourcebook

Skirmish Retinue Build

  • Choose an agreed points value. Around 500 points is a common starting skirmish.
  • At least 1 Commander must be chosen as the Retinue Leader.
  • Every Commander must be attached to a different warrior group to create a Command Group.
  • Max 50% of total points may be spent on Commanders + Command Groups.
  • Min 10% of points must be spent on Green troops unless the faction overrides this.
  • Minimum group size: 4 Infantry or 2 Cavalry.
  • All warriors in a Group must have the same profile, abilities, and equipment.
  • Coherency: 2" for Infantry, 3" for Cavalry.
Building a Command Group: buy the warrior Group first, then add the Commander to that Group. Commanders may have different equipment and may purchase Command Group upgrades.

Core Principles

  • On a d10, 10 always succeeds and 1 always fails.
  • High dice are good; low stats are better.
  • All measurements are in inches. You may measure at any time.
  • LoS is drawn centre to centre of bases.
  • Distances are measured edge to edge.
  • Groups have no facing.
  • Groups must stay at least 1" apart, except when charging into melee.
  • When attacking, you normally target the nearest enemy in range and LoS unless a Morale check or rule allows otherwise.
  • Fractions are rounded up.

Game Round Sequence

  1. Initiative Phase: each player rolls 1d10, applying -1 per Morale Penalty on the Retinue Leader. Winner chooses who goes first.
  2. Optional Initiative Push: a Retinue Leader may spend up to 2 Actions to roll extra d10s for Initiative.
  3. Activation Phase:
    • Compulsory Activations first (most often Broken Groups trying to Rally).
    • Then players alternate New Activations in any order.
    • Players may pass in New Activations; 3 consecutive passes end the phase.
  4. Housekeeping Phase:
    • Check victory conditions.
    • Remove all Action tokens.
    • Non-Weary Groups remove 1 Morale Penalty.
    • Weary Groups test Morale; if they pass, remove 1 Morale Penalty.
    • Remove Weary markers and re-establish 1" spacing.

Actions, Reactions & Status

ActionQuick summary
MoveMove up to Movement distance.
RunMove up to double Movement in a straight line.
CombatCharge into melee or Shoot with missile weapons.
DefendBrace or take cover against a future attack.
CommandCommander attempts to give another Group an extra Action or Reaction.
SpecialUsed for special rules, abilities, traits, or scenario effects.
RallyBroken Group attempts to recover.
Reactions: if targeted and you have an Action available, you may usually Attack Back, Reaction Shoot, Shoot Back, Defend, or use a Command Action to enable a Reaction.
Weary: a Group becomes Weary as soon as it declares a second Action/Reaction in a round. No Group may ever take more than two Actions/Reactions in a round.
Broken: Broken Groups cannot make Reactions and must activate in the Compulsory Activations step to Rally.

Morale Penalties

  • Most commonly gained by taking casualties.
  • Each Morale Penalty gives -1 to Morale and Order checks.
  • A Group can never have more than 6 Morale Penalties.
  • If a Group already on 6 would gain another, it must immediately take a Morale test; if failed, it becomes Broken.

Command Notes

  • Commanders are attached to their personal Command Group.
  • A Group using a Command Action must usually pass an Order check.
  • The Action granted by a Command Action counts as an Action for the target Group, not the Commander.
  • If the target Group has already acted once, a successful Command Action will usually make it Weary.

Useful Table Reminders

  • Keep Action tokens, Status markers, and Morale dice beside the correct Group.
  • Groups block Line of Sight.
  • Terrain can slow movement, hinder or block LoS, and improve defence.
  • If a Group ends up out of coherency, its next activation must be used to restore it if possible.
  • Mounted Warriors use 25×50mm pill bases; Infantry use 25mm rounds.

King John Retinues – Faction Reference

Faction-specific side • quick rules reference for the retinues in King John
Retinue List-building notes Faction traits / core special rules Quick play notes
Feudal European
pp. 51–65		 Standard flexible England / France style retinue. Cavalry Warfare: First Charge – first charge of mounted Knights each game gains Close Ranks if not Weary and charging 6"+.
Infantry Screen – mounted Knights may draw LoS through one friendly infantry Group when charging; that infantry takes an Order check to let the Knights move through safely. 		Best for classic combined-arms forces. Knights hit hard, infantry can screen the decisive charge.
Flemish
pp. 66–79		 Infantry-heavy urban militia style force. Militia Drill – Groups with the Flemish Ability may re-roll failed Order checks to receive a Command Action.
Militia Training – Flemish Militia / Flemish Spearmen may re-roll Attack dice of 1 in their first melee each round if an unbroken friendly Group with Flemish is within 6" and LoS. 		Works best in mutually supporting infantry clusters. Strong discipline and group synergy.
Poitevin
pp. 80–96		 No Baron option; best commanders top out at Lords / Paladins. Paragons of Chivalry – if a Poitevin Lord or Paladin wins melee, nearby friendly Poitevin Groups remove 1 Morale Penalty or gain a bonus die in their next melee that round; if the Lord/Paladin becomes Broken, nearby Poitevins take a Morale Penalty.
Proud and Quarrelsome – once per round, a non-leader Poitevin Lord may use a Command Action to let a friendly Group within 6" and LoS take an Action. 		Excels when heroic nobles stay active and aggressive. Momentum swings around your chivalric leaders.
Mercenary
pp. 97–107		 Hirelings: may form Combined Retinues with Feudal European, Flemish, Poitevin, Medieval Scottish, and Medieval Welsh retinues.
Mercenary Retinue Leader may never be the Liege Lord. 		Incendiaries – Mercenary Groups claim / contest Objectives from 4" instead of 3".
Paid Hirelings – ignore the normal 10% Green troops requirement.
Mercenary Company – choose one company style for the retinue: Brabancon, Flemish, or Gascon. 		Reliable professionals. Easy to slot into another force. Excellent if you want fewer Green troops.
Medieval Scottish
pp. 108–122		 Instead of 10% Green troops, must spend at least 20% of points on Groups with the Rabble Ability. Exercitus Scoticanus – Rabble within Command range and LoS of a Scottish Baron/Lord roll an extra d10 on the Morale check to over-ride their Compulsory Action; alternatively, if they do not over-ride it and are Inspired, they gain +2 Attack dice when they Shoot or Charge.
Horseless Classes – replaces the normal Green troop requirement with the 20% Rabble requirement. 		Expect more unpredictable levies but strong support from nobles. Great mixed-arms flavor.
Medieval Welsh
pp. 123–137		 Up to one third of a Group may upgrade to Knifemen.
Welsh Kings / Knights have Chivalry; Welsh Lords and Nobles count as Knights. 		He Who Fights and Runs Away – after losing a Combat Action, a Welsh Group may voluntarily flee and become Broken before Morale checks are rolled.
No Shame in Defeat – Welsh Groups roll 2d10 and keep the highest when testing to Rally.
Knifeman upgrade – during a Charge or Attack Back, force the opponent to re-roll one successful Defence die per Knifeman. 		Skirmishing, terrain use, and tactical withdrawal are key. Easier than most to recover after breaking.
Outlaw
pp. 138–151		 May only ever form Allied Retinues.
Outlaw Retinue Leader may never be the Liege Lord.
Leadership choice matters: Outlawed Noble or Wolfshead. 		Desperate Men – if a Group rolls a natural 10 on a Morale check that would make it Broken, or on a Rally test, it passes and thereafter auto-passes future Morale checks that would make it Broken.
Outlaw’s Vendetta (Outlawed Noble leader) – when fighting an enemy Command Group in melee, treat it as having +1 Morale Penalty for Morale Melee Modifier purposes.
Wolfshead’s Ambush (Wolfshead leader) – hold back up to half your Groups and deploy them after setup, usually 10" away from enemies / objectives (or 6" if out of LoS). 		Plays very differently depending on leader choice: revenge-fueled noble strike force or ambushing bandit swarm.

Handy Faction Reminders

  • Mercenaries ignore the normal Green troop minimum thanks to Paid Hirelings.
  • Scottish do not ignore low-quality troops – they swap the Green requirement for a 20% Rabble requirement.
  • Welsh are unusually forgiving after a retreat because of No Shame in Defeat.
  • Outlaws are the most leader-dependent retinue in the book.
  • Poitevins reward keeping multiple noble leaders active on the table.
  • Flemish get best results when militia groups support one another closely.
  • Feudal European is the cleanest “default” list for classic King John era Anglo-French warfare.
